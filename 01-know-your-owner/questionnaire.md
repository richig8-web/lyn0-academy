# Identity Questionnaire — Week 1

Ten minutes. Answer like you're writing to someone you're about to hand your whole week to,
because you are. Write in the file you're reading, then paste the parts into `SOUL.md` and
`USER.md`.

There are no wrong answers. There are only vague ones, and vague ones get you a generic AI.

---

**1. What should your AI call you?**
Not what it should be called. What it should *call you*.

___

---

**2. Name your AI.**
A name, not "Assistant". It'll use it every message.

___

---

**3. One sentence: what is this thing for?**
Finish this: *"It exists to..."*

___

---

**4. What do you actually do all day?**
Be concrete. A title alone is useless to an agent.

___

---

**5. What's the machine it's running on?**
OS, how much RAM, whether it's your work laptop or a separate box, whether it's always on.

___

---

**6. Which tools are already in your life?**
Email provider, calendar, notes app, cloud storage, spreadsheet, whatever you actually use.
This is how your AI stops suggesting software you don't have.

___

---

**7. What are the three things you hate doing?**
The boring, repeatable, drain-your-soul tasks. These become Mission Packs 4, 7 and 9.

1. ___
2. ___
3. ___

---

**8. What do you need it to never touch?**
Health documents, someone else's stuff, that folder with the tax returns, a person's number.
Be blunt here. This is the section that saves you from an apology later.

___

---

**9. Who else is in your life that it should know about?**
Partner, kids, parents, the dog, the colleague who sends you everything by voice note.
Names and the one fact that matters about each.

___

---

**10. How do you want to be talked to?**
Straight? Blunt? Handled? Talked to like a colleague? Talked to like an employee?
What makes you close the chat and what makes you mute it?

___

---

**11. What are you actually trying to get your life back?**
The real answer, not the presentable one. This becomes the north star for all 12 weeks,
and the sentence you put at the top of the landing page later.

___
